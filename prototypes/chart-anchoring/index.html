PROTOTYPE, throwaway

Do personal marks stay on the right chart content?

Draw ink, write text, or paste a saved stamp onto the chart. Then transpose it, resize the window, edit the source as a bandmate would, or paste a new version over it. Each mark should stay on the lyric, chord or beat it was put on. A mark whose content was deleted, or whose place a pasted-over version cannot prove, goes to the tray. It must never land somewhere else.
